Topic rules

Several problems: highest severity wins; tie → first specific one. Positive review → first praised feature; general praise = other.

Paid plan mentioned ≠ billing. A control is billing only if the review says it's premium-only/paywalled; otherwise usability (lyrics availability → catalog).

Ads → usability. Crashes, lag, connection → playback. "Not working / bad app" with nothing specific → other.

Intent: boycott slogan with no product complaint or stated leaving → unclear.

Severity: stars, angry tone, a crash alone or cancellation intent never raise it. Hedged harm stays below 5 and sets needs_review.
